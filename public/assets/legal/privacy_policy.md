## 1. Information Eduma handles

Depending on the features you use, Eduma and its service providers may process:

- **Account information:** name, email address, username, profile image, account
  identifiers, authentication tokens, and account settings.
- **Your content:** notes, canvases, spaces, files, images, recordings,
  transcripts, links, comments, study materials, and content you choose to share.
- **AI requests:** prompts and the text, images, audio, or other context you
  select for AI, transcription, or study-generation features.
- **Device and service data:** push-notification token, device platform,
  notification payloads, app preferences, and operational request information
  needed to sync and secure the Service.
- **Optional integrations:** Google account identity and, when you connect
  Google Drive, selected Drive file metadata and content; WhatsApp account/link
  information and messages or files you choose to send to the Eduma bot; and
  information needed to manage a plan or hosted checkout.
- **Device-local information:** files you pick, local calendar entries you
  choose to read or write, offline copies, and local preferences. These may stay
  on your device unless you use a feature that syncs or uploads them.

The app has no analytics or crash-reporting SDK use identified in its client
source or dependencies. **Eduma**

## 2. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate you; provide and sync your content; share
content with the people and spaces you select; operate AI, search, file, study,
notification, and billing features you request; protect accounts and systems;
respond to support requests; and meet legal obligations. Eduma should not use
private content for advertising. **Eduma**

## 3. Providers and disclosures

The client communicates with Eduma's API and uses these provider integrations:

| Provider / service | Data that may be disclosed when used |
|---|---|
| Google Sign-In | Google ID token for authentication; Google-provided account identifiers and profile claims needed to create or access an Eduma account. |
| Google Drive API | Google access token, selected file/folder identifiers and metadata, and file content requested for preview or download. Public link previews may request public metadata without signing in. |
| Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service | App/device push token and platform; push messages and notification payloads addressed to that device. Push is optional and can be turned off in settings. |
| Google Fonts delivery | Font file requested by family/hash and ordinary network metadata such as IP address, when a selected font is not cached and cannot be served by Eduma. |
| YouTube public preview endpoints | A YouTube video URL/identifier for an optional title preview, plus an image request for its thumbnail. Opening a video transfers you to YouTube or your browser. |
| WhatsApp | Your WhatsApp account/phone identifier when linking, and messages/files you choose to send to the Eduma bot. WhatsApp and the bot operator may process them under their own policies. |
| Hosted payment provider (not identified in this client) | Payment and billing details entered on the provider's checkout or portal, and plan/transaction information returned to Eduma. The provider and exact fields must be identified before publication. |

Eduma's server also receives account information, content, uploads, and feature
requests needed to provide the Service. AI requests are sent to Eduma's server;
the server-side AI provider(s), their locations, retention, and training
practices are **not verifiable from this client repository** and must be
disclosed by the service operator.

We do not sell personal information based on the client code reviewed. Providers
may act as service providers/processors or independent controllers depending on
the integration. **Eduma**

## 4. Storage, security, and retention

The app stores some session credentials in platform-protected storage and
content/preferences in local app storage for offline use. Server-side storage,
encryption, backup, and retention periods must be confirmed by the operator: **Eduma**

Use the in-app account-deletion control to request deletion. The operator must
document which server records are deleted, which must be retained by law, and
when backup copies expire. No internet-connected service can guarantee
absolute security.

## 5. Your choices and rights

You can manage push notifications, Google Drive/WhatsApp links, and certain
preferences in the app; disconnecting an integration stops future access where
supported. You can request access, correction, export, or deletion by using
available account controls or contacting [support@eduma.com.ng](mailto:support@eduma.com.ng).
Rights and response times depend on where you live. **Eduma**
