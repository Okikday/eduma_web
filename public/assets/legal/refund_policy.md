# Eduma Refund Policy

> **Draft for owner and legal review.** Eduma's client requests hosted checkout
> links from its backend; the payment processor and applicable purchase
> conditions are not identified in this repository. Complete each
> `Eduma` marker and confirm all mandatory consumer rights before publishing.

**Effective date:** Eduma

**Service provider:** Eduma, operating Eduma

**Support:** [support@eduma.com.ng](mailto:support@eduma.com.ng)

## 1. Purchases and billing

Plan descriptions, prices, billing periods, renewal terms, taxes, and the
payment provider should be shown before you confirm a purchase. Payment details
are entered with the hosted provider; Eduma's app does not contain a direct
payment SDK. The final version of this policy must identify the provider and
explain whether subscriptions renew automatically.

## 2. Cancellations

You can manage or cancel an eligible subscription through the billing portal
linked from Eduma, or through the marketplace/provider that processed the
purchase. **Eduma** Cancelling a subscription does not itself
refund a payment.

## 3. Refund requests

Contact [support@eduma.com.ng](mailto:support@eduma.com.ng) with the account email,
transaction reference, purchase date, and reason for the request. Do not send
full card numbers or security codes. If a marketplace or payment provider
processed the transaction, you may need to request the refund through that
provider.

**Eduma**
Nothing here limits a refund, cancellation, or other right that applicable law
requires.

## 4. Contact

For billing questions, email [support@eduma.com.ng](mailto:support@eduma.com.ng).
